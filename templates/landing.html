What it does

From sound to decision

Live monitoring

Analyses the microphone in rolling segments, so a detection appears while the sound is still happening rather than after the fact.

Upload and review

Drop in a recording and every segment is classified, stored and made searchable, with the waveform and spectrogram kept alongside it.

Rules and alerts

Severity, repeat counts and configurable rules decide what becomes an alert, what is logged quietly, and what a human should look at.

Manual review

Anything uncertain is queued for a person to confirm or correct, and the decision is written back into the event history.

How it works

Three steps, every time

  1. 1

    Capture

    Audio arrives from the live microphone or an upload and is split into fixed-length segments.

  2. 2

    Classify

    Each segment is scored across all ten classes by two models that never see each other's answer.

  3. 3

    Decide

    Agreement, confidence and your rules set the severity, the alert and whether a human is asked to check.

Cross-checking

Two models, one verdict

A server-side model and a browser-side model classify the same audio independently. Where they agree, confidence is high. Where they disagree, the event is flagged rather than quietly guessed at.

Server

Python model

Runs on extracted audio features, stores its full score vector with every detection.

Browser

Teachable Machine

Runs in the page on the same audio, giving a second opinion that is never shown the first one.

Questions

Before you start

Which sounds can it recognise?

Ten classes: {% for name in classes %}{{ display_name(name) }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}. Every segment is scored against all ten, not just the winner.

Does my audio get uploaded anywhere?

No. The server runs on your own machine and the browser model runs inside your own page. Recordings and detections stay in the local database.

What happens when the two models disagree?

The event is marked as a disagreement and routed to manual review instead of being reported as a confident detection.

Who can see what?

Accounts carry a role — admin, operator, reviewer or maintenance — and the pages, actions and exports available to you follow from it.
